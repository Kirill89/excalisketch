OpenMoji-black-glyf.ttf

All emojis in this font are designed by OpenMoji – the open-source emoji and
icon project. It is bundled with excalisketch and used by `tool render` to draw
emoji as monochrome line-art (so PNG/SVG output is identical on every platform,
without relying on OS emoji fonts).
